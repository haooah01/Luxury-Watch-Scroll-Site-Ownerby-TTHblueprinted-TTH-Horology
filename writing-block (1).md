# PRODUCT PRICING
## Luxury Watch Experience Website

### Overview

The Luxury Watch Experience Website is a premium digital experience designed to express the aesthetics, craftsmanship, and design language associated with high-end horology. The proposed pricing structure offers three implementation tiers, ranging from a streamlined digital foundation to a bespoke brand experience.

### 1. Luxury Foundation — $799 USD

**Vietnam reference price: 20,000,000 VND**

A foundational package for establishing a refined digital presence inspired by luxury watchmaking.

- Luxury-oriented website layout and visual direction.
- Product information and image presentation.
- Consistent interface design aligned with the brand identity.
- Mobile-responsive experience within the agreed scope.
- Predefined page structure, functionality, and revision limits.

### 2. Luxury Signature Experience — $1,199 USD

**Vietnam reference price: 30,000,000 VND**

The signature package for brands seeking a more immersive visual and interactive product experience.

- Brand-oriented interface design.
- Structured and visually engaging product presentation.
- Enhanced motion design and purposeful interactions.
- Responsive refinement across screen sizes.
- Integration of agreed interface components and project-specific functionality.

### 3. Luxury Bespoke Experience — $1,999–2,499 USD

**Vietnam reference price: 50,000,000–65,000,000 VND**

A tailored package for projects requiring a higher degree of customization and a distinctive digital identity.

- Bespoke experience design aligned with the brand's positioning.
- Specialized visual layers and interactive elements.
- Customized product discovery journeys.
- Advanced functionality subject to technical feasibility and agreed requirements.
- Testing, optimization, and handover against predefined acceptance criteria.

### Pricing Disclaimer

The prices above are indicative reference prices for website design and development services. They do not represent the retail, market, or appraised value of any physical watch.

A formal quotation depends on the agreed scope, functional requirements, content assets, third-party integrations, delivery timeline, and post-launch support arrangements.

**Core positioning:** A digital experience that communicates the spirit of luxury horology through design, structure, and interaction.
